    Common mistakes

      Start slow, around 60 BPM. Real songs run about 120 to 130. This is a simplified guide: directions and hand holds vary between teachers, so check the details with yours.

      What you see on the stage
      You, the leader
      Her, the follower
      Gold ring: joined hands
      Gold arrow: where your left hand goes next
      Shadow: the foot carrying the weight
      Outlined foot with L or R: your next step
      Dashed foot: a tap, no weight
      Floor arc: who turns, and which way

      How it works

      Two figures dance each move slowly, one count at a time. You are the green one.

      Practice a move

      1. Pick a move from the list. Start with the basics.
      2. Step through the counts with the arrows and read the cue for each one.
      3. Press Play at a slow tempo and dance along. Speed up when it feels easy.
      4. Switch to “Common mistake” to see what to avoid, and to POV to see it through your own eyes.
